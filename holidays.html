Public holidays

Public holidays in the Irad Special Economic Zone are fixed by the Holidays and Commemorative Days Act of 1993, revised in 2008. There are nine non-working public holidays. A holiday that falls on a Saturday or Sunday is observed on the next working day.

Religious festivals

Employees may take up to three days of paid leave a year for religious festivals that are not public holidays, such as Orozo Ait and Kurman Ait, or Orthodox Christmas on 7 January.

Flags

The national flag is flown on government buildings and schools on public holidays, and at half-mast on 3 February and on 15 August, the Remembrance Day of the Lost Tribes. Flag status today.
