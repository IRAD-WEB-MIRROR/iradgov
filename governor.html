Erlan Toktarov

Governor of the Irad Special Economic Zone since 2007. Chairman of the Democratic Party of Irad.

Official statement

Since July 2024 the Governor has been on an extended health retreat and continues to work in seclusion. Under the Elective Governor Acts the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat, conducts the day-to-day business of government on the Governor's instructions. Executive directives continue to be issued in the Governor's name, and his addresses are broadcast on IradOne.

Biography

Erlan Toktarov served as an officer in the logistics service of the Soviet Army before entering public life in Irad. In 1994 he was elected to the Territorial House of Representatives, and he later moved to the Territorial Senate, where he was Majority Leader from 2000 to 2004.

In the winter of 2006–2007 the cabinet of the short-lived Democratic Republic of Irad secretly negotiated with the three neighbouring states a set of treaties that would have dissolved Irad and divided it among them. On 25 February 2007, in Nurzhan, Erlan Toktarov made the drafts public, proclaimed the Irad Special Economic Zone and suspended a sovereignty that others had tried to sell. The words first heard that day, Irad satylbayt (“Irad is not for sale”), have stayed with the country ever since.

He took office as Governor after the Bishkek Ceasefire of September 2007 and was elected in the first regular ISEZ election in November 2011. He was re-elected in 2015, 2019 and 2023. Under his administration the Toktogul basin and the Fergana Mountain Transit Highway corridor were joined to the ISEZ by the 2009 corridor protocol, the som was restored to full reserve cover at 8.40 to the dollar, and the districts were reorganised in 2016.

A horseman since childhood, the Governor is patron of the Akyel Breeding Sanctuary and of the preservation of the Akyel and Chongtor breeds. His golden-dun stallion Zharkyn has appeared on the ISEZ's postage stamps.

Addresses

The Governor's addresses are recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. They are delivered in Iradi; the English texts below are issued by the Department of Public Information.

24 August 2026Greeting on the Feast of St. JanylRead the textClose

The Governor greeted the pilgrims at Iyık Janyl and thanked the Archeparchy, the city and the UNMI liaison staff who arranged the crossings for pilgrims from the tribal regions.

My brothers and sisters, pilgrims who have walked and ridden to Iyık Janyl, and all who keep this feast at home.

For three hundred years the bell of St. Janyl's church has called our people home. It called the herders down from the summer pastures and the townspeople up the hill; it called the clans across every line that history has drawn. Today it calls us still, and I give thanks that so many have answered.

I thank His Grace the Archbishop and the Archeparchy, the city of Iyık Janyl, and the liaison officers who arranged the crossings so that families from the mountains could pray beside families from the valleys. That is how it should be on this day.

I wish every pilgrim a safe road home, and every household a blessed feast.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

11 March 2026Irad Day addressRead the textClose

On the 34th anniversary of the proclamation of the Republic, the Governor spoke of the Toktogul refit, the school building programme and the Naryn Protocol renewal due in 2027.

People of Irad, in the cities and the valleys, in the mountains and abroad.

Thirty-four years ago, in a theatre in Talas, our people declared that Irad would be free. Those who met that night had no army and no money. They had a song, a banner with a white horse, and the courage of the Thousand Saddles. Everything we have built since stands on what they did.

This year the units of Toktogul will give our country more power than ever before. New schools are opening in Talas, Nurzhan and Iyık Janyl, built so that their lights stay on when the grid is busy. These are not gifts. They are the work of engineers, teachers and builders, and I thank them.

Next year the Naryn schedule falls due for renewal. Water and power bind the two banks of every river in Irad, and they bind us to our neighbours. We will meet that renewal as we have met every one before it: with patience, with respect for those who hold the mountains, and with the interest of every family who depends on that water.

Irad is one country. Its sovereignty is held in trust until all its people can decide its future together. On this day I ask every Iradi to remember that, and to be worthy of the star that gave our land its name. Happy Irad Day.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

25 February 2026Address on the 19th anniversary of the Special Economic ZoneRead the textClose

The Governor recalled 25 February 2007 in Nurzhan and thanked the workers of Nurzhan and the Chu valley.

Nineteen years ago, in Nurzhan, the people of Irad learned that their country had been offered for sale. On that day we said that no one would sell Irad, and we suspended a sovereignty that others had tried to spend.

The workers of Nurzhan stood at the gates of their refinery and their stations that winter. The farmers of the Chu valley kept the bread coming. I have not forgotten, and Irad has not forgotten.

The Special Economic Zone was created to keep our country whole until it can decide its own future. It has given us nineteen years of peace on the Chu, a stable som and work for hundreds of thousands. We will keep it so.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

31 December 2025New Year Address 2026Read the textClose

The Governor thanked the doctors, engineers and soldiers on duty on New Year's night and wished every family health and peace.

Dear compatriots. The year that ends tonight asked a great deal of us. When our country was spoken of abroad as something that could be bought, Iradis of every language and every side of the line answered with one voice. I was proud of you.

Tonight, while we sit at our tables, doctors are on duty in our hospitals, engineers are watching the turbines of Toktogul, and young soldiers are keeping the posts in the snow. Let us remember them.

I wish every family in Irad health, peace and a warm home. May the new year be kind to us all. Happy New Year.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

24 August 2025Greeting on the Feast of St. JanylRead the textClose

The Governor greeted the faithful and thanked the Sisters of the Cross and the St. Luka Mission for their clinics in the mountains.

To all who have come to Iyık Janyl, and to all who keep this feast in their homes and yurts: peace be with you.

Janyl was a mother who led her people through hard country to a place of safety. Our mothers have done the same in every generation. On her feast I think of them, and of the Sisters of the Cross and the doctors of the St. Luka Mission, who carry care into the mountains where no road goes.

May the feast bring every family together, and may the road home be safe.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

11 March 2025Irad Day addressRead the textClose

In the weeks after remarks made abroad about the future of Irad, the Governor said that Irad had never been for sale and never would be.

People of Irad. Thirty-three years ago our people proclaimed a republic in Talas with a song and a white banner. This year we have heard, from far away, that our country might be traded like a cargo. You answered in the streets of Talas and Nurzhan, in the bazaars and at the football grounds, in Iradi and in Russian and in English: Irad is not for sale.

You were right. Irad was not for sale in 2007, when it was offered in secret, and it is not for sale now. Our sovereignty is not a thing to be sold, by anyone, at any price. It is held in trust for the people of Irad, all of them, on both banks of every river.

Our government has said so through the proper channels, calmly and firmly. Let us now return to our work, our families and our fields, with our heads high. Happy Irad Day.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

25 February 2025Address on the 18th anniversary of the Special Economic ZoneRead the textClose

On the anniversary of the 2007 proclamation, the Governor recalled the slogan first heard in Nurzhan: Irad is not for sale.

Eighteen years ago, on a cold morning in Nurzhan, the workers who heard what had been promised away answered with three words: Irad satylbayt. Irad is not for sale.

Those words are being spoken again this month, in our streets and by our children. I ask only that they be spoken as they were in 2007: with dignity, with unity, and without hatred for any people.

The Special Economic Zone exists so that no government, at home or abroad, can dispose of our country. That is as true today as it was on the day we proclaimed it.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.

31 December 2024New Year Address 2025Read the textClose

The Governor thanked the people of Irad for their support during his health retreat and said that the work of government continues under the Lieutenant Governor's daily direction.

Dear compatriots. This year I have spoken to you less often than I would have wished. Many of you have sent letters, prayers and good wishes for my health. I have read them, and I thank you.

The work of government has not paused. The Lieutenant Governor has directed it day by day, on my instructions, and the ministries, the budget and the services that every family relies on have continued without interruption. Our agreements with our partners stand.

I wish every family health, warmth and peace in the new year. Happy New Year.

Recorded at the Governor's residence and broadcast on IradOne. English text issued by the Department of Public Information.
