The Governor's week

Public engagements of the Governor of the Irad Special Economic Zone for the current week, published by the Protocol Department every Monday.

2 November – 8 November 2026

  1. Monday 2 November
    • 10:00
      Weekly meeting of the Office of the Governor
      Government House, Talas
      Chaired by the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat, on the Governor's instructions.
  2. Tuesday 3 November
    • 14:00
      Academic council of Talas State University
      Talas State University
      The Governor was represented by the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat.
  3. Wednesday 4 November
    • Weekly reports of the ministries and departments
      Governor's residence
      Not open to the press.
  4. Thursday 5 November
    • 11:00
      Visit to the State Stud Farm of Talas
      State Stud Farm of Talas
      The Governor was represented by the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat.
  5. Friday 6 November
    • 10:30
      The Bank of Irad's quarterly report
      Governor's residence
      Postponed. A new date will be announced.
    • 16:00
      Executive directives and appointments for signature
      Government House, Talas
      Signed by the Lieutenant Governor, acting on the instructions of the Governor.
  6. Saturday 7 November

    No public engagements.

  7. Sunday 8 November

    No public engagements.

The programme may change at short notice. All times are Irad Time. Press accreditation for engagements open to the press: press@iradgov.org, by 15:00 on the day before.

Official statement

Since July 2024 the Governor has been on an extended health retreat and continues to work in seclusion. Public engagements are carried out by the Lieutenant Governor, Aidar Maksat, on the Governor's instructions.
