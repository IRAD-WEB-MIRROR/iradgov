Flying the national flag

The national flag is flown under the State Symbols Act 1993. It flies daily on Government House, the Mejlis, district and city administrations, courts and Gendarmerie posts, and on schools during the school year. Citizens, businesses and organisations are encouraged to fly it on public holidays.

On half-mast days the flag is first raised briskly to the top of the staff, then lowered slowly to half the height of the staff, and before it is taken down at sunset it is raised again to the top. Where the flag cannot be lowered, a black ribbon is tied to the top of the staff. No other flag may fly higher than the national flag, and flags flown beside it are lowered with it.

Half-mast days are set by the Holidays and Commemorative Days Act and by proclamation of the Governor. Proclamations are announced by the Office of the Governor's press service and read on IradOne.
