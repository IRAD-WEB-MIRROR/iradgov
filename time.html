Irad Time

The official time of the Irad Special Economic Zone is Irad Time (IRT), six hours ahead of Coordinated Universal Time (UTC+6). It was fixed by the territorial Standard Time Act of 1993. Summer time was abolished in 1996: the clocks are not changed in spring or autumn.

Irad Time is used by all state bodies, the courts, schools, the railways, the air terminal and the bus companies. Official use is the 24-hour clock. Because of the time zone, the sun is highest over Talas at about 13:10.
