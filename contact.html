Letters from citizens

Write to the Governor or the Lieutenant Governor at letters@iradgov.org or by post to Government House. Every letter is registered and answered within 30 days.

The reception room for citizens is open on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 10:00 to 13:00. Please bring your ID card.

You can also send an appeal online through e-Irad, or post or hand in the written appeal form OG-1 (PDF), which every state body accepts.

Public services

For passports, permits, business registration and utility bills, use e-Irad or visit your district office or city hall. The Office of the Governor cannot process applications.
