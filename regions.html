Cities and districts

The Irad Special Economic Zone covers about 29,400 km². It comprises three cities of governorate significance and six districts (aimak). Each city has an elected council and a mayor appointed by the Governor; each district is headed by a district head appointed by the Governor.

One country

The Government of the ISEZ regards Irad as one country, of about 217,400 km². Pending a settlement that all its people can accept, the mountain and steppe regions beyond the Qara-Chik line are governed by their clan councils under customary law. Matters across the line are dealt with through the United Nations Mission in Irad, the bodies of the 2007 Bishkek ceasefire and the joint technical commission of the Naryn Water and Power Protocol. Travel permits issued by clan councils are registered by the Republican Gendarmerie at the crossing points.
