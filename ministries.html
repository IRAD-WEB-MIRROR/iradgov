Ministries and departments

The Government of the Irad Special Economic Zone has ten ministries, each headed by a minister appointed by the Governor, and two technical departments, each headed by a director. The Lieutenant Governor chairs the cabinet. The ministries of Health, Education, and Labour and Social Protection report to the Lieutenant Governor; the other ministries and the departments report to the Governor.

How the ministries were formed

Under the territorial administration the executive was organised in departments, each headed by a Secretary appointed by the Governor. In December 2006 every department became a ministry and every Secretary a minister. Executive Directive 2007/09 of 6 August 2007, “On the organisation of the administration”, kept the ministries and formed two technical departments: the Department of Infrastructure and Communications, from the former ministries of Communications and of Public Works, and the Department of Energy. Older documents and the territorial fonds of the State Archives refer to the present ministries as “the Department of…”.

Minister: Almaz Turgunbaev

The State Defense Corps, the armed forces of the ISEZ, formed in July 2007. Chief of the General Staff: Maj. Gen. Ruslan Atakanov.

Formed in December 2006; the territorial executive had no defense department.

Reports to the Governor

Correspondence through the Office of the Governor

Ministry of Economy

Minister: Dinara Usupova

Economic policy; the register of firms and trade licensing; the State Tax Service; the Territorial Statistics Office; Iradstandart, the State Agency for Standards, Metrology and Certification; the Foreign Labour Quota Section.

Reports to the Governor

Correspondence through the Office of the Governor

Ministry of Natural Resources

Minister: Chingiz Asanov

Mining concessions under the 1995 code and ore licensing; the Forestry and Protected Areas Service (Besh-Tash National Park and the Irad part of Aksu-Zhabagly); the Plant Quarantine Inspectorate; tailings and mine safety.

The territorial Department of Natural Resources until December 2006.

Reports to the Governor

Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources

Minister: Melis Nurmatov

Crop and livestock production; the regional agricultural directorates, among them the Talas Basin Agricultural Directorate and its State Veterinary Inspectorate; irrigation canals, canal rotations and water plans for dry years.

Reports to the Governor

Ministry of Health
