Press releases

Releases from the Office of the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor, the ministries and the agencies of the ISEZ. Accredited journalists may contact the press office of the Department of Public Information.

2026

20 releases
  • 2 November 2026DistrictsLower Naryn District Administration
    Lower Naryn District to Complete Water Storage Tank for Rim Villages in 2027 Budget

    Following a Talas State University household survey in Korgoncho, Sopol-Tepa and Kyzyl-Oktyabr, the Lower Naryn District Administration has announced that it will fund the completion of the water scheme's storage tank in the 2027 budget.

  • 30 October 2026Lieutenant GovernorOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Investment in Sary-Bulak District's Mining Sector Reaches US$216.6 Million

    On a working visit to Sary-Bulak District on Thursday, Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat was informed that investment in the district's mining sector has reached US$216.6 million. Investors have indicated that about 2% of their sales will go towards district roads, schools and clinics.

  • 29 October 2026MinistryMinistry of Economy
    Record Harvest Lifts ISEZ Farm Output to IS 6.1 Billion in Nine Months

    The Ministry of Economy's preliminary estimate puts ISEZ agricultural output for January to September 2026 at IS 6.1 billion, with volume up about 12% on the same period of 2025. The result is one of the pillars of the 9.4% growth reported by the Bank of Irad.

  • 27 October 2026Lieutenant GovernorOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Lieutenant Governor Presents Plan for a Refinery at Kochkor-Ata to Serve the Southern Districts

    Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat told residents of Lower Naryn District that the government wants a second, smaller refinery at Kochkor-Ata to bring fuel closer to the southern districts. He described it as a plan to be studied, not a decision.

  • 27 October 2026MinistryMinistry of Natural Resources
    Record 700 Participants Attend Nurzhan Oil and Gas Conference

    The annual Nurzhan Oil and Gas Conference, hosted by NurGaz and opened by the Minister of Natural Resources, closed on 23 October with about 700 registered participants, some 40% more than in 2025.

  • 19 October 2026Lieutenant GovernorOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Lieutenant Governor visits the construction site of the Nurzhan Compute Park

    Aidar Maksat and the Director-General of Attelkom, Samat Kasymov, inspected the first phase of a planned US$450 million data-centre hub in Nurzhan's Eastern Industrial Zone.

  • 19 October 2026GovernmentISEZ Investment Promotion Office
    88 investment agreements worth US$2.4 billion signed in three years

    The first full tally of investment agreements since late 2023 shows energy, antimony and logistics drawing most foreign capital.

  • 16 October 2026MinistryMinistry of Agriculture and Water Resources
    Draft order on a temporary halt to table potato imports published for comment

    After a record harvest of about 210,000 tonnes, the ministry proposes to stop imports of table potatoes until 1 March 2027. Comments are open until 30 October.

  • 15 October 2026GovernmentTerritorial Statistics Office
    Census of business entities begins: enumerators visit firms, farms and stalls until 30 November

    The first count of all business entities in the ISEZ runs from 15 October to 30 November. Answers are used for statistics only.

  • 14 October 2026GovernmentOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Preliminary harvest figures: 1.2 million tonnes of grain and 210,000 tonnes of potatoes

    The Chu and Talas valleys have brought in one of the strongest harvests in years. Grain is up by about two-fifths on last year.

  • 12 October 2026Lieutenant GovernorOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Phased restart announced for the Sary-Bulak Antimony Works and the Chatkal smelter

    Speaking to residents and plant staff in Sary-Bulak, the Lieutenant Governor said idle lines will return in stages, with the first stage expected to create around 600 jobs.

  • 12 October 2026GovernmentISEZ Customs Service
    Customs roadmap with Kazakhstan for 2027–2028 signed at Kyzyl-Adyr

    Electronic pre-clearance at the Talas North Terminal, a shared schedule of inspection hours and a separate lane for low-risk lorries.

  • 10 October 2026MinistryMinistry of Natural Resources
    Toktogul refit raises capacity to 1,440 MW

    Each of the four generating units now delivers 360 MW instead of 300. The Minister of Natural Resources reviewed the work at the Lower Naryn switchyard and the National Dispatch Centre.

  • 8 October 2026StatementDepartment of Public Information
    Statement on the restriction of websites offering unlicensed financial services

    At the request of financial supervisors, the website of the “Tulparcoin” token and the foreign platform on which it trades have been added to the list of restricted sites.

  • 5 October 2026Lieutenant GovernorOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Lieutenant Governor opens a solar-powered school in Navbahor, Nurzhan

    The 24-classroom school is the twelfth school or kindergarten completed this year. Eighteen more are due before the end of December.

  • 1 October 2026DistrictsLower Naryn District administration
    Magnitude 4.9 tremor in the Kara-Unkur valley: no damage reported

    The district emergency service checked bridges, schools and the switchyard within the hour. Residents of older mudbrick houses are asked to check walls and chimneys before the heating season.

  • 29 September 2026StatementBank of Irad
    Bank of Irad: payment services require a licence

    Any service that takes payments or holds funds for residents of the ISEZ needs a licence under the 1993 Act, whatever the instrument it uses.

  • 26 September 2026GovernmentOffice of the Lieutenant Governor
    Executive Directive 2026/31 sets at least IS 41 million for school textbooks

    A four-year printing programme for 2027–2030, expert review of every new title and a code on each copy showing the school and pupil it was issued to.

  • 24 September 2026StatementBank of Irad
    Bank of Irad statement on the token marketed as “Tulparcoin”

    The token is not legal tender, is not backed by the bank's dollar reserves and carries no deposit protection. Only the som and the US dollar are legal tender.

  • 24 August 2026The GovernorOffice of the Governor
    The Governor's greeting on the Feast of St. Janyl

    Governor Erlan Toktarov greeted the faithful and the pilgrims gathered at Iyık Janyl. The address was broadcast on IradOne.
