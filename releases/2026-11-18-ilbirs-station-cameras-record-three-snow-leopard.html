Ilbirs Station Cameras Record Three Snow Leopards in the Chatkal Highlands

The Ilbirs Station at Uuk-Bulak this week released camera-trap footage of three snow leopards crossing a snow-dusted slope in the Chatkal Highlands. The station's head said the sequence shows the camera line is working, while cautioning that it is not a count.

The Ilbirs Station at Uuk-Bulak, in Sary-Bulak District, this week released camera-trap footage showing three snow leopards crossing a snow-dusted scree slope in the Chatkal Highlands. The animals were recorded in a single sequence in the early morning.

The station is run by the Faculty of Biology and Chemistry of Talas State University, with veterinarians from the Talas Veterinary Research Center. It opened on 23 October, with the Ministry of Natural Resources represented at the ceremony.

Dr Aizhan Torobekova, head of the station, said the group may be a female with two grown young. She was careful to set out what the pictures can and cannot show. "It is one sequence on one morning. It is not a count, and we will not call it one," Dr Torobekova said.

She said the footage nonetheless shows that the cats are using the slope and that the camera line is doing its job. The cameras stand on tribal hill pasture and operate with the agreement of the clan councils, an arrangement the station has kept since it opened.

Researchers hope that further sequences through the winter will show how often the animals pass and whether the group stays together. The station will continue to review the camera cards as the snow deepens, and any further footage will be released with the same care about what it does and does not show.
