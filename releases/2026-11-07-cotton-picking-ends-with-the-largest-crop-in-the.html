Cotton Picking Ends with the Largest Crop in the Ministry's Records Since 2007

The Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources reports that the 2026 cotton picking in the Zone is nearly finished, with a raw-cotton crop of about 41,000 tonnes, the largest in its records since 2007. Steady canal water and a full Toktogul reservoir supported the season.

The Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources on Saturday announced that the 2026 cotton picking in the Irad Special Economic Zone is nearly finished. The raw-cotton crop is put at about 41,000 tonnes, the largest in the Ministry's records since 2007.

All of the crop was grown on the Fergana rim farm belt round Bazar-Korgon, Korgoncho and Sopol-Tepa in Lower Naryn District.

Minister of Agriculture and Water Resources Melis Nurmatov announced the figure and the end of picking. "I thank every farm family, every driver and every picker who stayed in the field until the last boll," the Minister said.

Steady water supported the season. The canals held through the summer, and the Toktogul reservoir stood 86.2% full on 6 November, so the rim farms did not face the irrigation rotas of 2021 and 2023. Alisher Kholmatov, head of the Lower Naryn District administration, noted that farmers in the district were able to plan their sowing, labour and loans with confidence in the water supply.

The cotton is ginned at the Xinjiang Lihua facilities in Chu District, and the cottonseed is pressed for oil at the Bazar-Korgon oil mill. Part of the fibre goes on to Zhetysu Industrial Textiles in Nurzhan. The Ministry thanks the farms, gin and mill workers and drivers whose work carried the harvest through to its close.
