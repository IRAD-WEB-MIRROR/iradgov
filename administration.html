The administration

The Government of the Irad Special Economic Zone is led by the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor. The Lieutenant Governor chairs the cabinet of ministers and directors of departments. The full directory of ministries and departments, with their remits and addresses, is on a separate page.

Office of the Governor

Governor

Erlan Toktarov

Head of the executive. On extended health retreat since July 2024.

Lieutenant Governor

Aidar Maksat

Chairs the cabinet and the ISEZ Security Council; conducts the day-to-day business of government.

Department of Public Information

Secretary of Public Information: Burulai Akylova

Government press office; registration of publishers and printers; performance and screening permits.

External Relations Office

Director: Nathan Kenjeev

Trade offices abroad; liaison with UNMI and the bodies of the 2007 ceasefire and the Naryn Protocol.

Public Integrity Commission

Chair: Gulzat Bekmuratova

Established 2014. Investigates misconduct by public officials and agrees settlements.

Investment Promotion Office

Kirill Zhdanov

Single counter for foreign investors; keeps the register of investment agreements.

Rural Development Fund

Director: Kanybek Zhorobekov

Co-finances small enterprises in the districts: workshops, small hydro stations, processing lines.

Education Quality Agency

Director: Cholponai Bekbolotova

Accredits university and college programmes (2009).

Ministries

Full directory

Ministry of Internal Security

Minister: Talant Mambetaliev

Police detachments in the six districts, the Republican Gendarmerie, passports and residence registration, the Corrections Directorate and the Civil Protection Service.

Ministry of Defense

Minister: Almaz Turgunbaev

The State Defense Corps, the armed forces of the ISEZ.

Ministry of Economy

Minister: Dinara Usupova

Economic policy, the register of firms and trade licensing, the State Tax Service and official statistics.

Ministry of Natural Resources

Minister: Chingiz Asanov

Mining concessions and ore licensing, forests and protected areas, plant quarantine.

Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources

Minister: Melis Nurmatov

Crops and livestock, the regional agricultural directorates and the State Veterinary Inspectorate, irrigation and water plans.

Ministry of Transport

Minister: Oleg Rudenko

Public roads, some 4,800 km in the zone, and road transport.

Ministry of Justice

Minister: Kapar Tilekov

Registration of charities, foundations and associations.

Ministry of Health

Minister: Dr Jamila Kubatova

Hospitals and polyclinics with the city governments, rural health posts, vaccination rounds; emergency treatment is free.

Ministry of Education

Minister: Kanykei Umotova

Head of the curriculum section: Dinara Seitkulova

Public schools, the school building programme and textbooks.

Ministry of Labour and Social Protection

Minister: Aikerim Kalbaeva

Social benefits, disability and children's allowances, help for migrant workers' families.

Departments

Department of Energy

Director: Sofya Kuznetsova

Energy policy; holds the state's shares in Iradenergo.

Department of Infrastructure and Communications

Director: Arstan Medetov

Posts and telecommunications, the state's stake in Attelkom and the e-Irad public services portal.

Agencies and services

Republican Gendarmerie

Commander: Maj. Gen. Ruslan Toktarov

The Qara-Chik line checkpoints, the Border Demarcation Brigade, the Highway Patrol Corps and the Special Protective Service.

State Defense Corps

Chief of the General Staff: Maj. Gen. Ruslan Atakanov

The armed forces of the ISEZ, formed in July 2007.

Civil Protection Service

Ministry of Internal Security

Director: Col. Gleb Tikhonov

Avalanche, mudflow, flood and glacier-lake warnings; earthquakes; pass closures.

State Tax Service

Ministry of Economy

Head: Nadezhda Kovalenko

Profit, income and sales taxes and pension contributions under the Tax Code 2009.

State Pension Fund

Director: Tamara Tentekova

Pensions, paid at Irad Pochtasy counters from the 10th of each month.

Customs Service

Director: Uson Yakupov

Deputy director: Anvar Sultanbekov

Customs posts at the frontier crossings; the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal.

Territorial Statistics Office

Director: Gulbarchyn Ryskulova

Official statistics; the 2018 census and the 2026 census of business entities.

Heritage Protection Service

Director: Navruz Khudoiberdiev

Protected monuments and open-air museums, including Rabat-i-Safed; excavation permits.

Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority

Director: Gulnara Tokonova

Supervises export escrow accounts and deducts royalties (1994).

Talas River Hydrological Monitoring Service

Director: Uzakbai Mukashev

River gauges, the glacier inventory and the basin forecasts for each irrigation season.

National Equestrian Sports Directorate

Director: Taiyr Kamchiev

Traditional horse sports, the Irad State Hippodrome and the pari-mutuel tote.

Irad Pochtasy

Director-General: Aizhan Mamatova

State postal enterprise: some 190 post offices, pension payments, newspaper subscriptions and stamps.

State Archives of Irad

Director: Mattay Sheraliev

Archive fonds since the Kokand charters; legal deposit numbers for publications.

Attorney General

Nurlan Tashtanbekov

Criminal prosecutions and applications to the courts on behalf of the Government.

Independent institutions

Bank of Irad

Chairman: Azamat Temirov · Deputy chairman: Ermek Sadykbekov

Issues the som under currency-board rules, at 8.40 to the US dollar since 17 March 2008; supervises banks.

Territorial Legislature (Mejlis)

Speaker: Nurbek Sultanov

60 members elected for four years in eight constituencies. Legislative Hall, Talas.

Supreme Court of Irad

Chief Justice: Ayyub Nazarbek

Five justices

Hall of Justice, Liberty Boulevard, Talas. Register of pending cases published online since October 2026.

Territorial Election Commission

Chairman: Daniyar Borubaev

Seven members

Voter registers, registration of candidates and results. Next general election: 2 November 2027.

State enterprises

Iradenergo

General Director: Toktosun Baitikov

State joint-stock company (2008): Toktogul and the Naryn cascade, the high-voltage grid, the National Dispatch Centre; Talas and Nurzhan Electric Networks.

Irad Pochtasy

Director-General: Aizhan Mamatova

State postal enterprise (2008): about 190 post offices, pensions and allowances at the counter, money orders. Head office: Talas Central Post Office.

Iradstrakh

General Director: Jypar Sartbaeva

State Insurance Company of Irad: compulsory motor third-party policies, property, crop and livestock insurance; about 60 offices.

Irad Temir Jolu

Director-General: Pavel Yugai

Railways: about 690 km of line from the Kazakh frontier through Nurzhan and Talas.

IradOne

Chairman: Alikhan Toktarov

State public broadcaster: IradOne, IradOdin and the Radio Irad network.
