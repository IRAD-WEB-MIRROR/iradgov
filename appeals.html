How appeals are handled

Every resident of the Zone may write to the Governor, the Lieutenant Governor or any state body with a proposal, a request or a complaint. Appeals are handled under the Directive on Appeals of Citizens, which binds the Office of the Governor, the ministries, the departments and the city halls.

How to send an appeal

  • Online, through the online reception on e-Irad.
  • By post or by hand, on the written appeal form OG-1 or as a letter, to Government House, Central Square, Talas, or to the body concerned. Every state body accepts form OG-1.
  • By e-mail to letters@iradgov.org (appeals to the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor).
  • In person, in the reception room for citizens at Government House on Tuesdays and Thursdays, 10:00–13:00, with your ID card.

Give your full name and postal address, say what you ask for and why, sign and date it. Attach copies, not originals.

What happens next

  1. The appeal is registered on the day it arrives, or on the next working day, and given a number. You can follow an appeal sent online by its number on e-Irad.
  2. If the question belongs to another body, the appeal is sent there and you are told where it went.
  3. You receive an answer within 30 days of registration, or within 15 days if no inquiry is needed. If an inquiry takes longer, the term may be extended once, by up to 30 days, and you are told why (item 14 of the Directive).
  4. The answer is sent the way the appeal came: by post, by e-mail or to the online reception.

Appeals that are not considered

  • Anonymous appeals, and appeals without a postal address for the answer (item 9).
  • A repeated appeal on a question already answered, if it gives no new facts (item 11). You are told this once.
  • Appeals that insult officials or threaten anyone.

Matters before a court are not decided by appeal: the court decides them.

If you disagree with the answer

You may appeal against the answer of a ministry, department or city hall to the Office of the Governor, stating the number and date of the answer. You may also take a decision of a state body to court; an appeal does not stop the time limits for going to court.

A complaint about the handling of an appeal (no answer, no registration, a late answer) goes to the Office of the Governor, Department for Work with Appeals of Citizens, by the same routes.
